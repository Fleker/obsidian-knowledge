---
A series of <span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">Commuter Rail</span> lines that go between <span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">Manhattan</span> and various places on <span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">Long Island</span>.

In 2023, it saw a 16% rise in ridership along with a record on-time performance [of 95.65%](https://x.com/LIRR/status/1877765452744814659).

There's also a plan for [Amtrak](./Amtrak.html) to [take a round trip](https://x.com/ThunderWolf08/status/1877918428020773330) from <span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">Washington DC</span> through to <span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">Ronkonkoma</span> through <span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">Jamaica</span> and <span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">Hicksville</span>.
