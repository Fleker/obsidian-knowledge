> The US statisticians Dublin and Lotka predicted in in 1936 that the maximum possible life expectancy would be 70 years. That was broken in 1941. Later, in 1979, the UN predicted that the maximum possible life expectancy would be 80 years. Their prediction was broken the next year by Iceland. Many other predictions have already been broken, as life expectancy has continued to rise.
