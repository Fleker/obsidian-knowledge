Supplemental Nutritional Assistance Program (<span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">Food Stamps</span>)

The US spends nearly [$200b annually](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/ag-and-food-statistics-charting-the-essentials/food-security-and-nutrition-assistance/?topicId=d7627f77-6cee-4ab9-bbb9-8c74d4778941#:~:text=Federal%20spending%20on%20USDA's%20food,year%202021%2C%20adjusted%20for%20inflation.) on food assistance. Biden [enabled a permanent increase in SNAP benefits](https://x.com/What46HasDone/status/1797837715221987651).

The WIC (Women, Infants, and Children) program helps give baby formula, but states grant one company an exclusive contract. This [leads to shortages](https://x.com/AlecStapp/status/1842510137258393642) if that company is problematic.

This program [maxes out](https://x.com/besttrousers/status/1942018389154631921) at $975/month for family of four.

* [Gillian Branstetter highlights the disparity in taxpayer spending, with Americans spending significantly more on corporate subsidies ($670/year) than on SNAP benefits ($36/year).](https://x.com/GBBranstetter/status/1984251732722114650)
* [Increased SNAP participation doesn't necessarily lead to higher grocery prices; in fact, prices may increase less in areas with higher SNAP take-up.](https://x.com/jdcmedlock/status/1982154758531002548)
* When Trump raised work requirement standard, people [in rural areas](https://x.com/fawfulfan/status/1953453089999978979) lost benefits and causing rural grocers go bust
* Poor families [spend more on food than they receive in benefits](https://x.com/captgouda24/status/1942053344257728988) 
* James Melock points out that food ovuchres [are a very American program](https://x.com/jdcmedlock/status/2051046436402495528)
