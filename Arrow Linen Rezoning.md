A rezoning of the Arrow Linen Supply Company in <span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">Windsor Terrace</span>, a place that has lost housing in the last year. Supported by NYS Senator [Zellnor Myrie](https://x.com/zellnor4ny/status/1833265325069967718). This is a [YIMBYism](./YIMBYism.html)-aligned plan. Windsor Terrace [lost housing in 2023](https://x.com/annemariegray/status/1831700400161927364) because apartments [are being turned into townhouses](https://x.com/OpenNYForAll/status/1831446313889886639).

A NY Daily News editorial [is in support of the project](https://x.com/bfurnas/status/1834962804685537399?t=sXvsAlGdsQoj5zUMQD2MTg&s=19). [[Open New York]] [is also supportive](https://x.com/OpenNYForAll/status/1889754619208749068).

## Brooklyn CB7 Meeting
There was a community meeting on <span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">2024-09-09</span> with [a lot of spicy takes](https://x.com/OpenNYForAll/status/1833275119000510821) from those [mostly white, old residents](https://x.com/KornbergMaya/status/1833284178395197922).

People are [telling their neighbors to leave](https://x.com/TheOmniZaddy/status/1833344954661265723).

Someone says schools [would get overfilled](https://x.com/MosesNYC/status/1833337792270680345) even as Brooklyn lost 4000 students since 2019.

Those who [are against](https://x.com/OpenNYForAll/status/1833342016786247741) more housing [are landlords](https://x.com/OpenNYForAll/status/1833346090634014935). They [deflect blame](https://x.com/OpenNYForAll/status/1833352034310820305) for affordability. They are harassing their own neighbors telling them to [leave the city](https://x.com/bfurnas/status/1833510408839434763).
