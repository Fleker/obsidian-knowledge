The **Bay Area Rapid Transit** system.

## Reducing Costs
A recent BART project [has doubled the amount of delivered cars](https://x.com/Noahpinion/status/1816572184590188616) by hiring people to work there and using their expertise, instead of farming it out to expensive contractors.

## Fare Evasion
By upgrading the fare gates, they [reduced evasion significantly](https://x.com/maxdubler/status/1814375182615945304) without involving police.
