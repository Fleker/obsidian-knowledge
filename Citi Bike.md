A bike share program owned by Lyft and operating in <span class="private-link" title="This page is private" style="color: #94a3b8; cursor: not-allowed; text-decoration: dashed underline;">Manhattan</span>. It's very successful, [setting a record 5.1M rides](https://x.com/CitiBikeNYC/status/1841232583339782249) in September 2024.

People can get [some money](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/09/19/nyregion/citi-bike-scam-nyc.html) moving bikes between docks.


> ["Lots of confusion in the comments and in general. Here is a breakdown:](https://x.com/ShabazzStuart/status/2017777715491766290)
>
The operating license/contract: City of NYCDOT
The contractor  & owner of the bikes/tech: Lyft
The day-to-day operator: Motivate LLC
The System Sponsor: Citibank"

* [Despite adverse conditions like extreme cold and snow, Citi Bike ridership remained high, indicating strong and inelastic demand for the service among dedicated commuters.](https://x.com/ccbaker2023/status/2021615549243936824)
