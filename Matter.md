A standardized application layer for the smart home.

## Matter v1.4
This updated spec [includes](https://www.theverge.com/2024/11/7/24289972/matter-smart-home-standard-1-4-spec-new-features-device-types):

- Enhanced multi-admin
- Improved integration with [[Thread]] border routers from different companies
- More devices for energy management: heat pumps, home batteries, solar panels

## Matter & Peer-Standards

* [Matter and OpenAD offline protocols are collaborating to facilitate automated demand response programs, allowing smart home appliances to adjust energy usage and potentially reduce costs by communicating seamlessly with utilities and the grid.](https://www.instapaper.com/read/2010975046)
