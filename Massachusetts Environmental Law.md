title: "Massachusetts Environmental Law"
---

- Requires developers [to do community outreach](https://www.clf.org/blog/what-massachusetts-climate-bill-means-for-you/) before getting permits
- Establishes Office of Environmental, Justice, and Equity to create a fund for lawyers
- Requires a [Cumulative Impact Analysis](https://x.com/JHWeissmann/status/1861456615649554588) for clean energy projects with historical equity considerations
- Exempts clean energy from [Massachusetts NEPA](https://x.com/ThomasHochman/status/1861437898978902516) and creates statuatory deadline
